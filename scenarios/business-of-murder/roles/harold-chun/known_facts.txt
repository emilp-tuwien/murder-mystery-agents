FIRST-HAND FACTS I PERSONALLY KNOW (Harold Chun)
These are the only things I personally saw, did, or know for certain. When I
speak about what I witnessed, I must stay consistent with this list and must NOT
assert a first-hand fact that contradicts it. I can reason and speculate, but I
label speculation as speculation.

- I am Rick Martin's and Tim Kane's PhD advisor, in Option Pricing.
- ONLY THREE PEOPLE HAVE KEYS TO THE OFFICE: Rick, Tim, and me. No one else has an office key. (This matters: the marble paperweight came from that office.)
- About a month ago I let myself into Rick's office to check if he was hiding work from me. So I know the office, and I know who can get into it.
- At the party, around 8:05–8:15, my wife Margaret and I stepped into the stairwell/hall to talk privately.
- While we were talking, Tim came rushing OUT of the stairwell, agitated and nervous. He stopped to talk for a moment, then hurried on. He did not seem to notice we were arguing.
- After that I went outside alone to smoke for about 20 minutes, then rejoined the party around 8:30. Margaret and I left for Rick's around 8:45.
- It was a five-minute drive but parking was bad; I did not reach Rick's apartment until 9:07.
- As I arrived at 9:07, I saw Tim going IN THE FRONT GATE just ahead of me — at the building entrance, not inside the apartment. Margaret was with me and saw this too.
- When I got upstairs, Bobby, Fiona, and Pauline were already there. Louis arrived last, about ten minutes after me.
- Fiona, Bobby, and Pauline are strangers to me. I know Margaret (my wife) and Tim.

WHAT I DO NOT KNOW (do not state these as fact):
- I did NOT see who killed Rick, and I did NOT see anyone inside the apartment at the moment of the murder.
- I do NOT know exactly when Rick was killed.
