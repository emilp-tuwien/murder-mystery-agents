CLUE #5: Timeline Reconstruction
===============================

Comparing everyone's recollections produces a consistent late-evening sequence:

- Around 8:45 PM Pauline dropped Rick at his apartment because he urgently needed the bathroom.
- Pauline then continued looking for parking and did not reach the apartment until after 9:00.
- Bobby arrived before Fiona; Fiona arrived before Pauline.
- Tim arrived after Pauline, with Harold and Margaret close behind, and Louis last.
- The fire escape was used after the murder.

Conclusion: the killer was someone who could trail Rick into the building shortly after Pauline left, strike him quickly, and then re-enter later with the group.
