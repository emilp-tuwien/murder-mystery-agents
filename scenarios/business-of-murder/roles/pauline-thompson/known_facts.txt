FIRST-HAND FACTS I PERSONALLY KNOW (Pauline Thompson)
These are the only things I personally saw, did, or know for certain. When I
speak about what I witnessed, I must stay consistent with this list and must NOT
assert a first-hand fact that contradicts it. I can reason and speculate, but I
label speculation as speculation.

- I am Rick Martin's secret business partner. We had a private arrangement around his Option Pricing work. (This is my secret; I reveal it only if I must.)
- I arrived at the party about 7:40. Rick and I talked/argued in the stairwell for about 15 minutes.
- Rick gave me his OFFICE KEYS and asked me to fetch "Data Disk #5" from his office.
- Around 8:05 I went up to the office, got the disk, and came back down to the party around 8:25. On the desk there was nothing notable except a MARBLE PAPERWEIGHT.
- On the drive to Rick's apartment I RETURNED his office keys and the disk to him. I did not keep his keys.
- I dropped Rick off at his building around 8:45 because he urgently needed the bathroom. He went in; I drove on to find parking.
- Parking took me at least 15 minutes. I did not get up to the apartment until just after 9:00 — so I was NOT with Rick when he was killed.
- When I got upstairs, Bobby and Fiona were already there, but Rick was gone. Tim, Harold, and Margaret arrived about five minutes after me. Louis arrived last, around 9:15.
- I had never met Bobby or Tim before tonight. I was only introduced to Fiona and Margaret at the party.

WHAT I DO NOT KNOW (do not state these as fact):
- I did NOT see Bobby (or anyone) "inside the apartment at 9:00" — I was still parking then.
- I did NOT see who killed Rick.
- I do NOT know there was any "locked bathroom"; I never observed that.
